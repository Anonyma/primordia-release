Small experiments can change the trajectory of a scientist, a tool, or an organism. Primordia exists to make those experiments possible, visible, and connected to people who can help them grow.

What happens after you’re funded

Start by completing your digital signature and submitting your banking details so your grant can be wired. Clarify your project’s starting point, then join the WhatsApp cohort group for updates, quick questions, and conversations between calls.

For the welcome session, prepare a five-minute introduction: what you’re testing, why it matters, how you’ll measure success, and where you need help.

Over the next four months, run your experiment, document your decisions, and track your spending. The sections below explain the support available, how to share progress, and what to deliver at the end.

Support along the way

Bring your blockers to office hours

Sessions run every other week, with two time slots: Europe and APAC at 10:00 CET, and the Americas at 18:30 CET. Add the sessions to your calendar and bring questions or problems you’d like to work through.

Tell us who could help

Ask for technical troubleshooting, scientific feedback, business development, fundraising, partnerships, or publication support. We aim to connect each project with at least one mentor, advisor, or contact who can help with its specific needs.

Share your progress

You don’t need to become a content creator. Share what’s happening, and we’ll help more people find your work. After you’ve signed, our public-comms lead, Tom Fraczak, will explain how we can help.

Announce your grant

Post on LinkedIn and X about what you’re working on and why it matters. Tag @PrimordiaGrants so we can share it, and add “Primordia Grant Recipient” to your LinkedIn experience.

Send a short update each month

Share what you did, what you saw, and what changed. A lab photo, an early result, or an experiment that didn’t work is enough. Keep it honest and understandable.

Fill out the monthly check-in sheet too. We use it to highlight your progress across Primordia’s channels.

Grant essentials

Use the funds for the approved project

Follow the approved budget, keep expenses identifiable, and retain receipts. Stay within the approved research scope, location, biosafety level, and applicable legal or ethics requirements.

Tell us before making major changes to your scope, methods, team, location, or budget. We can usually work through reasonable pivots.

Finish with results others can use

Provide a final technical and results report, plus final financial accounting. Make all or a substantial portion of your results public through a report, preprint, dataset, protocol, or code. We can help explore a DOI and indexing for your output.

Acknowledge Primordia, ValleyDAO, and Biopunk Lab where relevant. Primordia may publicize non-confidential information about your award.
